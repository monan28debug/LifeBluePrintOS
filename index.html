Welcome to Life Blueprint OS

Your entire life — health, money, goals, skills, career & more — organized in one beautiful dashboard.

What is this?

A personal life management system that runs 100% in your browser. No cloud. No subscription. No tracking. Fully private.

📦
What can I do?

Track health, set goals, manage money, log skills, plan your career, write notes, schedule events, and get AI-powered suggestions — all in one place.

🔒
Is it private?

Yes. Everything saves only on your device inside your browser. Nobody else can see your data — not even us.

⚠️
⚠️ Important — Data Safety Warning

Your data lives only in this browser on this device. If you clear your browser data, reset your phone, or switch browsers — your data is permanently erased with no recovery possible.

👉 Always use the 💾 Export button to download a backup regularly.

💾
How to backup & restore?

Export ⬇️: Click 💾 in the top bar → downloads a database.db file to your device.

Import ⬆️: Click 💾 → Import tab → select your saved .db file → data restored instantly.

💾 Data Management — Import & Export

⚠️
Your data is stored ONLY on this device

It is NOT backed up anywhere online. These events will permanently delete your data with NO recovery:

  • 🗑️ Clearing browser data / cookies / site data
  • 📱 Resetting or changing your device
  • 🌐 Switching to a different browser
  • 🕵️ Using private / incognito mode
  • 🗄️ Uninstalling the browser
⬇️

Export (Backup)

Download your entire database as a .db file to your device. Do this regularly!

⬆️

Import (Restore)

Select a previously exported .db file to restore all your data on this device.

💡 Pro Tip: Export your data at least once a week. Save the file to Google Drive, iCloud, or email it to yourself. This is your only backup method.

⚠️ IMPORTANT — Read Before You Start

Your data is saved ONLY in this browser on this device. It does NOT sync to any server or cloud. If you:

  • Clear browser data / history / cookies
  • Reset or change your device
  • Switch to another browser or incognito mode
  • Uninstall this browser

→ All your data will be permanently deleted with no way to recover it.

✅ Solution: Use the 💾 Save button (top bar after login) regularly to download a backup file to your device.

💾 How Your Data Works
🗄️
Where is data saved? Inside your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) on this specific device and browser only.
⬇️
How to Export (Backup): After login, click the 💾 button in the top bar → click "Export My Data" → a database.db file downloads to your device. Save it to Google Drive or your phone gallery.
⬆️
How to Import (Restore): Click the 💾 button → click "Import Data" → select your saved .db file → all your data is restored instantly on this device.
🔄
Transfer to another device: Export on Device A → send the .db file to Device B → Import on Device B. Your data moves with you.
🔒
Is it private? 100% yes. No server receives your data. Your entries never leave your device unless you manually export them.
